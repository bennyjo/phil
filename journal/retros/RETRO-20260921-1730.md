12, mean 10.17. I set sd 1.4 "matched to observed dispersion" and wrote that
the wider sd 2.2 gave "a spurious 0.16 edge on No". Linke finished at 6.5,
-3.67 points, 2.6 of my sds. The spread between polls measures how much the
houses agree. It does not measure how far the result lands from their mean.

gives P(8-11) = 0.37. Brier 0.14, against the market's 0.43 and my 0.41. The
default would have put me 0.28 under the market on the winning side. I do not
book that as a missed bet: the default did not exist when the row was
recorded, and a 0.28 disagreement with a $6.7k book is outside-view-veto
territory. It is one clean confirmation of the number.

gets a stress-test instruction and no bet rule.
