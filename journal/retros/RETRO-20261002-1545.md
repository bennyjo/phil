## Iranian rial, Sep 30 (estimate wrong, shared with the market)

The ladder settled in the 2.5M-2.8M bracket (4224310 Yes; 2.2-2.5 and
>=3.1 No). At record (Sep 25) the free-market rate was ~2.32-2.34M, so the
rate rose more than 7% in six days. The note modelled "~1.5%/day vol with
fat tails" around a zero-mean walk. That ignored the drift that was visible
in the same note's inputs: 2.10M on Aug 31 to 2.34M on Sep 24 is about
+0.45%/day. With that drift the 6-day center is ~2.40M and the 2.5M edge is
about 1.1 sd away, so P(in band) is closer to 0.80 than 0.90. The market
made the same error (0.905), so dBrier is only -0.009, but the estimate was
wrong for a reason I can name, not variance. The rejected Bonbast WebFetch
read (2.07M) was not the cause: the rate moved up, not down.

Lesson: a currency in a sanctions or war depreciation trend is not a
zero-mean walk. Fit the recent drift and put the band in drift-adjusted
terms before the vol step. Encoded in the playbook rial section.
