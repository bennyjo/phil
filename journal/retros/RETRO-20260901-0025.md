## Grading the pre-registered touch-family test (2026-08-18 note)

The 2026-08-18 recording note asked for two things at settlement: (1) how
the three vetoed legs (WTI 95, WTI 90, Gold 4600 — already won earlier)
graded against the veto boundary, and (2) whether the ~38% WTI / ~22% Gold
annualized-vol inputs were "in the right neighborhood."

**(1) Veto boundary:** both newly-settled vetoed legs (WTI 95, WTI 90)
would have **won** their No-side counterfactual bet had the veto not fired
— same shape as the family's existing losing pattern (Yes-side self-model
1W/6L per the ledger before today). Combined with Gold 4600 (already won,
settled Aug 21), that's now 3 of 3 vetoed legs in this specific batch where
the veto declined a bet that would have paid off. This does **not** argue
for lifting the veto — the veto's job is avoiding the self-model's
over-claimed *directional* edge, and on this batch the self-model actually
had the right direction (low touch probability, correctly) three-for-three;
the boundary just also blocks the (rarer) times the self-model is
overconfident in the wrong direction, which is what the family's broader
1W/6L Yes-side record still argues happened more often historically. n=3
new counterfactual wins is not enough to overturn a rule built on a larger
loss sample — logged for the ledger, no policy change.

**(2) Vol-input accuracy:** weak evidence, not conclusive. WTI never
touched any of $70/75/90/95/100 across the whole window — consistent with
*some* annualized vol at or below ~38%, but "stayed inside a $30 band
around an $82 spot" is a wide, low-information test (many vol values would
have produced the same non-touch outcome for all five barriers
simultaneously). Gold is more informative: spot got to ~$4696 against a
$4700 barrier (missed by $4, per the 08-26 revision note) — closer than
the original 22%-vol model's 13.9% touch estimate would suggest was likely,
hinting 22% may have been a slight *under*-estimate of realized vol rather
than accurate. Single instance, not enough to revise the input; flag for
the next occasion this family is used (prefer a measured-vol source over
the guessed 22%/38% figures if one becomes reachable, per the existing
unvalidated-sd caveat).

**Net effect on category evidence:** commodities-touch settled
brier_delta=-0.0594 this run (score.py) — the family beat the market on
