internal waters) before September 15 noon ET, and No if it does.

The entry rationale quoted that exact sentence and then concluded the
opposite: "day 1 would be 09-02, needing through 09-15 ... arithmetically
impossible for Yes regardless of anything since." It treated the
market's end date as the resolution horizon. The clause says the end date
bounds when the LAST strike may occur, not when the window must close.
Every subsequent checkpoint (eight of them, including two WebSearch
re-checks "confirming" the No thesis) re-confirmed the fact and never
re-read the clause. The fact was right; the mapping from fact to outcome
was wrong; and the position's edge class, `info-race`, was a fiction:
there was no information the market lacked.
