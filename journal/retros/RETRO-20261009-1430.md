  sd 6). The outcome sat in the bracket my mean pointed at, but sd 6 spread
  mass into the 25-30 and 35-40 neighbours, so I under-priced the modal
  bracket (0.32 vs book 0.40) and over-priced the 35-40 tail (0.15 vs
  0.075). The book's ladder was tighter than mine and was right. A
  self-judged sd with no constituency poll is a guess; the three
  pre-election projections (32-38) already bracketed the answer, and their
  spread (~3 pts) was a better sd than my 6. Same shape as the RBI
  modal-leg rule (modal leg priced closer to the book wins). Lesson: on a
  by-election share ladder with several independent projections, use the
  projections' own dispersion (floor ~4) as sd, not a round 6.
