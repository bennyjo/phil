## Lesson (encoded)

Second graded instance of the seat-model family (AfD Thuringia Sep 7 was
the first). AfD: adopting the model and not blending toward the market
was right. Quebec: the model's leader median was 3 seats high and the
book, priced at the low end of the model range, was right. Together
(n=2): a seat model is mechanical for STRUCTURE, but the leader's seat
median can sit a few seats high when the polls overstate the leader.
When the market sits inside the published model range, the row is
no-edge at the model, and I should not lean outward toward the outlier
models. This is already the "price-inside-range -> no-edge" rule, which
I applied on the skip but not on the recorded number. Playbook edit:
for a seat-model row where the book sits inside the published model
range, record the probability of the track-record-best single model
(here Qc125 0.39 / 127qc 0.31-0.39 -> ~0.35), not a blend that adds the
outlier models.
