### Downstream test (operator pointer, PR #18)

The question: is the no-bet streak caused by research washing out the screener's directional
signal?

- **Method.** I took October screener collects since 10-20, grouped by hour, and kept the top 15
  by divergence. That gives 381 distinct markets. I fetched fresh gamma resolutions into a
  scratch cache; it is NOT committed, and `journal/screener-outcomes.jsonl` was last checked on
  2026-09-12. Of the 381, 236 have resolved. Scoring uses screen_replay's split:
  delta = null + excess, with null = (p-m)^2 and excess = 2(p-m)(m-y). Scoring is on the winner
  side, per distinct market, and not event-clustered unless stated.

  | Set | n | Excess | Null | Delta |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Screener, all escalated | 236 | -0.028 +/- 0.016 | | +0.044 |
  | Screener, the 137 that research rowed | 137 | -0.052 +/- 0.024 | 0.085 | +0.032 |
  | Research, the same 137 | 137 | -0.036 +/- 0.013 | 0.022 | -0.014 |
  | Research, clustered (81 crude event clusters) | 81 | -0.032 +/- 0.013 | | |

- **Readings:**
  - **The screener's direction is real (this matches the operator's -0.076), but its size is
    about 3x too big.** Its optimal scale is k = -E/(2N) ≈ 0.31, so its delta is positive.
  - **Research keeps about 70% of the screener's directional excess** at a quarter of the null
    cost. Its mean |divergence| is 0.095, against the screener's 0.26. Research's own optimal
    scale is k ≈ 0.82, so its shrinkage toward the mid is already about right. **No new shrink
    or anti-shrink rule is warranted.**
  - Research leans the same way as the screener on only 78 of 137 rows. It is not echoing the
    screener.
  - **About half of research's excess is AAA gas.** One cluster of 12 rows contributes -2.33 of
    the total -4.9. Without AAA, research's excess is about -0.02 per row, which is at the book
    in betting terms.
- **Conclusion.** The streak is not a research defect. Research beats the mid by about 0.014
  Brier per row, and that edge lives mostly in wide books (AAA, say-the-word) that the spread
  gate correctly refuses. The streak reflects the opportunity set, not effort. This test has
  caveats:
  - Research mids were taken at record time, not at screener time.
  - The clustering is crude, built from question text.
  - n=137 in one 9-day window.
