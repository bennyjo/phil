## Lesson (no estimation-rule edit)

When a PortWatch rung is forecast with 0 published in-window days and the
book disagrees with the base-rate model by >.25, the book's divergence is
itself evidence of in-window information. This is an estimation rule
("shade toward mid when 0 window days and gap > .25"); the rule budget is
full (7 SHADOW lines in score.py) and a 0-day half-gap rule was already
tried in October, so I do not open a new rule. Recorded here for the deep
retro: one data point (n=1 decisive row), below the ~15 threshold.
