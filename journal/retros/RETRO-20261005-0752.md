## Grading

The batch splits into two pairs.

1. **Flávio 2nd (`22d7ab1cf2f3`, `59feffcbfcb5`).** Both were wrong on the
   ordering. They assumed Lula finished first (about 0.85 on Sep 23, 0.75
   on Sep 26). Flávio finished first and Lula second. The Sep 23 leg is
   the mirror of `b350adc7e95c` (Lula 2nd at 0.15, which resolved Yes), so
   the two legs were coherent with each other and wrong together. The
   market (0.72, then 0.745) was less wrong. This pair costs 0.212 of
   Brier against the market and is the whole reason the batch lost.

2. **Renan Santos 3rd (`2b497632d5f9`, `c9e0968700d5`).** Both beat the
   market. Santos was not third. The later 0.45 read (recorded 23:28Z on
   Oct 3) superseded the 0.55 read. The 0.55 read had a 0.04 No-side edge,
   below `min_edge`, so no bet. The 0.45 read is outside-view-veto with a
   No-side counterfactual that would have won.

The 8%+ row (`a5900944fde5`) and the PL Senate row (`42cf449f72d1`) are
small. The PL row was a no-edge skip, and the market was right on the Yes
outcome, so the skip is not a loss.

## Why the Lula-first read was wrong

The Sep 23 and Sep 26 Flávio-2nd legs used the same paired-poll margin
mean of about +2 for Lula. RETRO-20261005-0527 already flagged a pro-Lula
poll error history. The Sep 26 note cited a 9-point 2022 miss toward
Bolsonaro and a realistic margin sd of 4.5, but still centred the margin
on Lula +2.5. The bias was known and the centre was not moved by it. The
0527 retro found the late rows shaded toward Flávio and the count went
further. This batch fits that reading: the shading was directionally right
but the early centre was too far toward Lula.
