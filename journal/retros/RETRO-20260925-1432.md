## Why the revision from 0.62 to 0.76 worked

The first pass (`78786fdc3015`) priced in a structural tail risk from
the 2021 Duma election, where United Russia lost the party-list
plurality in 4 regions (Sakha, Mari El, Nenets AO, Khabarovsk), without
checking whether those specific regions were even holding elections this
cycle. The revision (`e31cac471a14`) did that check against the actual
2026 39-region roster (en.wikipedia) and found none of the 4
historically-weak regions on it — the roster instead skews toward
tightly-managed ethnic republics (Chechnya, Dagestan, Bashkortostan,
Ingushetia, Tuva, Mordovia, Chuvashia, Karelia, Adygea) with a
2024/2025 track record of sweeping official results. Dropping the
inapplicable tail risk moved the estimate from 0.62 (near market-agrees)
to 0.76 (a real 0.08 edge vs the 0.68 ask), and the market ultimately
proved the narrower read right.

**Lesson for regional/constituency-sweep markets in managed elections**:
when a prior cycle's exceptions are the basis for this cycle's tail-risk
estimate, check whether those exact regions/constituencies are even in
this cycle's roster before carrying the risk forward — a historical
exception rate is not a property of the country, it is a property of
the specific units that produced it. n=1 event, one country: recorded as
a confirmed instance of an underused check, not a new gate.
