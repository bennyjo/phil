## Was the estimate wrong, or variance?

Every row resolved No in a month-end that stayed inside a range (BTC
roughly 84k-87.4k from Sep 21, never touching 87.5k or 82.5k). So the
batch is close to one macro draw, and the own-versus-market split is
explained completely by which side of the mid I sat:

- Own ABOVE the mid: 7 decisions (D1-D5, D7, D14), market closer on all 7.
- Own BELOW the mid: 8 decisions, own closer on all 8.

The 7 above-mid reads were all near barriers (gap under 10%) on windows
of 3.3-9 days, read at 30-day realized vol (0.41-0.69). Where the note
also quoted a short-window realized vol, it was far lower (BTC 6d ~0.18
on `f29090ac1de8`, ETH 6d ~0.2 on `01564feeaec3`). The 30-day window still
carried the early-September moves; the last week did not. That matches
the DEEP-2026-09-24 pattern (own above market on 4 intraday dip reads,
market closer on 3) and RETRO-20261001-0020's two commodity month-end
touches (Silver, Gold), both above the mid and both lost. So the
estimates on the above-mid side were wrong in the same direction, for a
nameable reason: the vol input lagged a quieter regime.

The far-barrier split is the opposite case: the book prices far
barriers above the driftless tool (noted in RETRO-20260921-2044), and
this month that premium was not earned (5 of 5 own closer). The dBrier
gains there are small (-0.002 to -0.024) because both sides sat low.

## Lessons and edits

1. Playbook touch section: tally appended with the counting choices
   above, so the next grade cannot re-count these rows.
2. Pre-registered test, not a rule yet (evidence: 7 of 7 above-mid near
   reads lost this batch, plus DEEP-2026-09-24's 3 of 4 and the two
   month-end commodity rows): on every near-barrier touch row with a
   window of 10 days or less, the note also records `touch.py` at the
   7-day realized vol. The next re-grade compares the 30-day read and
   the 7-day read against the outcome. This makes the regime-lag
   hypothesis checkable instead of a story.
3. Process miss: the Sep 28 weekly settlements had no retro. Settlement
   grading is never deferred (schedule.json rule); the cause is the
   divergence, where the cycle that settled them may have been on either
   branch and the other branch's resolve saw them already settled. That
   belongs to the divergence repair, already in proposals.
