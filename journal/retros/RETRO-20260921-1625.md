## Strategy edits (`strategy/playbook.md`, seventh data point)

1. **Default sd.** Vote-share Gaussian brackets use sd 3.0 for a state or
   regional election and sd 2.5 for a national one (n=2, a floor, not a fit).
   This replaces the interim "sd >= 1.8". Points, not percent of the share.
2. **Centre stress test replaces the sd sweep as the robustness check.** A
   bracket bet must keep edge >= min_edge with the mean moved to the most
   recent poll AND one further point along the poll trend. An sd sweep alone
   is void as evidence for any bracket that does not contain the mean.
3. **No bet against the trend.** No Yes bet on a bracket, and no bet on an
   ordinal leg, that needs the party to REVERSE its trailing poll trend,
   unless a sourced event explains the reversal. Evidence: both German-set
   legs (-$10), and the direction of all five German misses with a trend.

What I did not change: `risk.json`. politics-general bets stand at n=6, win
0.50, pnl +$47.62, brier_delta -0.0239. One $5 loss at a 0.09 entry is inside
normal variance for the category, and n is far under 15. The error was in the
method, so the edit is in the method.

One consequence to flag: a wider sd RAISES tail-bracket probabilities, so
edit 1 alone would have made this losing bet look better (P 0.20 at sd 3.0).
Edits 2 and 3 are what would have stopped it. They ship together on purpose.
