confident pick (25C, 44%) lost. The model's confident-ish pick (not 25C,

mean) was directionally right. That is the *opposite* result from KL, where
the market's skeptical pick (30C unlikely) won and the model's confident
pick (30C, ~27%) lost.

So the two real disagreement rows in this category — KL and Amsterdam — now

verdict, it's a wash.** Neither hypothesis (a) nor (b) is supported over the
