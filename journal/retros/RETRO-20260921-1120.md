## Where my number was actually wrong

My 2021-baseline branch had UR +7 vs NL +9 as a near coin flip, built on a
UR seat centre of 331 from the VCIOM pre-election forecast (UR 51-53 pct
list). UR landed near 58 pct list and 208 SMDs, 355 seats. The error was a
24-seat undershoot of the ruling party, not the clause.

This is the second Duma election in a row where the state pollster's
published forecast understated UR's official result: 2021 VCIOM forecast
41-44 pct vs 49.8 official; 2026 VCIOM forecast 51-53 vs about 58. On
2026-09-18 I applied the 2021 pollster-miss history to LDPR (overstated) in
the third-most rows and did not apply the mirror image to UR. The book did:
it priced UR 340-354 at 0.24 and 355+ at 0.055 where I had 0.15 and 0.04
(`71dc5127a964`, `4c8153f75a72`, both still open, both `unvalidated-method`
and correctly never bet). On the preliminary 355 count those two rows will
be graded at the 354/355 boundary - do not pre-book either.
