## Lessons -> playbook edits

- **Stale-read rule (new):** a RYD/YouTube count identical across two fetches, or unchanged
  over a window where the trailing pace predicts >=0.05M of growth, is a stale cache. Do not
  project from it and do not re-record a snapshot on it; keep the previous snapshot or record
  at the mid.
- **Decay band floor (new):** when projecting a multi-day view count, the decay assumption
  is the *measured* day-over-day change of the trailing-24h adds (>=2 full days observed),
  not a band of guessed rates. With fewer than 2 measured day-over-day ratios, the row sits
  within 0.05 of the mid (no measured decay, no off-mid view).
