## Lesson and edit

**Price-inside-the-model-range rule (playbook, election section).** When my own
model prints a range across defensible input choices (poll weighting, error
sd, recency window) and the market price lies inside that range, the claimed
edge is an input choice and the row is `no-edge`. A bet needs the price to lie
outside the whole range by at least min_edge. Evidence: 7ec71e307f12 (range
0.55 to 0.665, price 0.665, bet lost; the six Berlin reads above). The same
test was applied correctly on 506bc4c8087e (sd sweep 0.80 to 0.96 versus ask
0.77: the price was outside the range, and that row won).

**Label consistency.** A market I declined as `unvalidated-method` stays
declined on that method until a settled row validates it. A smaller edge four
days later is not new evidence. Encoded in the same paragraph.

No risk.json change: min_edge was not the failure. The edge was mis-measured,
not too small.
