inside the same session, and a window that averages over 14 quiet days
cannot see that. A close-above row recorded hours ahead should scale the
remaining-session sd from the day's own realized range when the day's
move already exceeds about 2 window-sds, and shade further when the move
is directional toward the barrier.

Superseding at 17:33Z was the right call and is the reason both rows
score close to the market. The lesson is encoded in the playbook (this
