## Was the estimate wrong, the fill bad, or variance normal?

**Singapore 33C is the informative row, and it is an estimate error, not
variance.** The open-meteo point forecast for Changi was 33.0C. The actual
high WAS 33C (the 33C bucket won, the 32C bucket lost). The model put only
0.323 on its own mean because sd=1.2C is a next-day forecast error, and the
row was recorded at 04:18Z, which is 12:18 local time at Changi with
roughly half the diurnal curve already observed. The market at 0.845 was
pricing the partial-day observation my once-daily point forecast does not
see. The forecast note itself flagged this exact caveat ("already noon
locally", "market likely pricing partial-day observed temps") and then
recorded the unconditioned number anyway. That is the failure: a known
structural mismatch was written into the ledger as an honest belief.

**Singapore 32C (02:22Z, 10:22 local)** is the same failure one bucket
over: the model spread 0.233 onto 32C from a 33.0C mean; the market had it
at 0.07. Same root cause (next-day sd applied intraday), smaller cost.

**Shanghai 32C** went the model's way: forecast 29.6C at Pudong, market
0.355 on 32C, actual not 32C. This is the market being wrong about a

the three where the model's information (a sharp point forecast) beat the
market. n=1, and it was recorded at 10:22 local, so it carries the same

## Lesson and playbook edit

The weather-Gaussian method has two regimes and the playbook only had one:

1. Next-day rows (recorded before the resolution day starts locally):
   sd=1.2C is at least the right shape. Record: KL L, Amsterdam W, both
   next-day.
2. Same-day rows (recorded after local sunrise on the resolution day):
   the market conditions on observed temperatures and the point forecast
   does not. Record today: 1W/2L, and the two losses were on the buckets
   the point forecast itself pointed at. Applying sd=1.2 here is
   miscalibrated by construction, not by bad luck.

Edit (playbook, weather section of the veto table ruling): same-day
weather rows must be conditioned on the observed partial-day maximum
(open-meteo hourly with `past_days=1`, or its `current` field) with the
bucket sd shrunk to 0.6C after local noon; if that observation fetch
fails, the row gets NO forecast (benchmark-unreachable, per the standing
"never invent an estimate" rule) rather than the unconditioned next-day
number. Category stays no-bet.
