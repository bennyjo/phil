## Process finding: the row broke the touch-family ruling twice

The touch-family gate closed on DEEP-2026-09-01 with two instructions:
touch rows are `unvalidated-method` forecast-only, and guessed-vol inputs
are retired (measured realized vol, or implied vol from an adjacent ladder
rung). This row, recorded 13 days later, used a guessed 3pct daily vol
("This vol is guessed, not sourced", in its own note) and carried the
`outside-view-veto` label. Neither choice changed the decision, since both
labels are no-bet. They do change the bookkeeping:

- The label put a touch-family row with a 0.04 edge on the veto
  counterfactual ledger, where it now counts as a veto "win". It is there
  because the mechanical ledger keys on the label (same treatment as
  `9618a7d0872d`, the Aug 24-30 sibling). It says nothing about the veto
  boundary: a 0.04 edge was never a veto-sized disagreement.
- The guessed vol means the row does not count toward the ruling's
  "re-grade at 6 settled measured-vol rows" bar.

This is a pattern, not one slip. Of the 6 crypto touch rows recorded
since the ruling that carry a modeled estimate, 4 used an unsourced vol:
`dde658c37455` ("60pct annualized", no source), this row (guessed 3pct
daily), `a28637cb4026` (swept 25-50%) and `8d1eb46b7c32` (swept 50-90%,
still open). Only `fde4324641b4` and `854536ded8be` named a measured
source. A rule that half the rows miss needs a mechanism, not a restated
sentence. `strategy/tools/touch.py` (new in this commit) is the
reflection formula with a mandatory `--vol-source`; it reproduces
`854536ded8be`'s recorded 0.87 from that row's inputs (0.8733) and
refuses a bare "guess". It also prints the estimate at 0.75x and 1.25x
the vol, which is the sensitivity check this row did by hand.
