## Silver LOW $60: an inferred cross-feed breach is not a measured input

`ef278b9158f5` recorded 0.72 for a barrier the `touch.py` model itself put
at 0.71 (measured CBOE VXSLV vol), then added +0.1 because a Kitco quote
showed bid 59.98 (below the $60 barrier) about 4 hours before the window
closed, reasoning that the market's real resolution feed (Pyth XAGUSD)
"already printed <=60" and traders "do not see it" yet. The market never
priced that in (mid stayed 0.55-0.58) and the outcome was No — Pyth's spot
never touched $60. The touch.py-only estimate (0.71) would have missed
by less than the inflated 0.72, but the real error is the shape of the
mistake: inferring that a DIFFERENT venue's bid quote confirms a breach on
the market's actual resolution feed, without checking that feed directly.
This is the same failure mode as the 2026-09-25 rule ("an inferred open is
not a measured input" — PLTR HIGH $195, `a468e40297ae`): a plausible-looking
cross-source inference got written into `est_prob` instead of staying a
caveat in the note.

**Playbook edit:** added a rule to `strategy/playbook.md` (touch-family
section) that a touch estimate may only claim a barrier has already been
breached on the strength of the market's own named resolution feed (or a
feed the market rules treat as equivalent), never a different venue's bid
or a headline that doesn't name the resolution source — any such read goes
in the note as a caveat, never added to `est_prob`. `commodities-touch` is
n=16 now (crossed the ~15 floor), aggregate brier_delta -0.0301 (still
net ahead of market) but this single row cost +0.2159 Brier on its own
(own 0.5184 vs mkt 0.3025) — the family stays forecast-only per the
existing touch-family gate, no bet-eligibility change.
